# INC005 - Bad Context

## Customer Report

Customer asked for the refund window and received incorrect policy information.

## Customer ID

INC005

## Request

"What is the refund window?"

## Expected Response

Refund requests are accepted within 30 days of purchase.

## Actual Response

"Refund requests are accepted within 60 days of purchase."

## Impact

The customer received incorrect policy information even though the request completed successfully.

Low - controlled lab incident.

## Status
